# Victorian Design - 1880s British Arts & Crafts

Refined 19th-century aesthetic inspired by **Victorian-era Britain** (1880s-1890s), channeling William Morris's Arts & Crafts movement, ornate industrial typography, and elaborate railway signage.

## Historical Context

**Period**: Late Victorian Era (1880-1895)
**Location**: Britain
**Movements**: Arts & Crafts, Gothic Revival, Industrial Ornamental Design

## Design Elements

### Typography
- **Cinzel** (Display) - Classical roman capitals, inspired by imperial inscriptions
- **Libre Baskerville** (Body) - Refined transitional serif, late 18th-century style
- **IM Fell English** (Ornate) - Based on Bishop Fell's 1670s types, old-style numerals
- ALL CAPS for headings with generous letter-spacing (0.08-0.12em)
- Old-style numerals for temperatures (characteristic Victorian typographic detail)

### Color Palette
Muted, dignified tones from Victorian textiles and interiors:
- **Cream** (#F5F1E8) - Parchment/aged paper
- **Tan/Bronze** (#D4C4B0, #8B7355, #6B5D4F) - Oxidized metals
- **Burgundy** (#6B2737) - Victorian textile accent
- **Forest Green** (#2F4538) - Deep natural dye
- **Gold** (#C9A961) - Gilded details
- **Rust** (#A0563B) - Heat systems
- **Teal** (#4A6D6D) - Cooling systems

### Ornamental Details
- **Double borders** (3px double stroke)
- **Corner brackets** - Decorative L-shapes framing titles
- **Embossed texture** - Diagonal repeating lines (pressed metal effect)
- **Section symbol** (§) - Used as navigation indicator
- **Gradient backgrounds** - Simulating aged parchment
- **Engraved badges** - Metallic gradient with inset highlights
- **Dotted patterns** - Radial gradients in corners

### Materials & Textures
- **Pressed metal plates** - Buttons with gradients and inset highlights
- **Aged parchment** - Subtle cream-to-tan backgrounds
- **Brass/bronze** - Gold accents and borders
- **Engraved lettering** - Text shadows simulating depth
- **Woven fabric** - Diagonal stripe patterns

## Vocabulary

Victorian-appropriate nomenclature:
- "Domicile" (not "Home")
- "Chambers" (not "Dashboard")
- "Chronicle" (not "History")
- "Third Storey" / "Ground Floor" / "Cellar" (British floor names)
- "Dormant" (not "Off")

## Typography Scale
- **Header**: 32px Cinzel, 700 weight, 0.08em spacing
- **Floor titles**: 22px Cinzel, 700 weight, 0.12em spacing
- **Card names**: 16px Cinzel, 700 weight, 0.08em spacing
- **Temperatures**: 42px IM Fell English (old-style nums)
- **Body text**: 11-13px Libre Baskerville
- **Small caps**: 9-11px for badges and labels

## Layout
- **Centered chapter headings** with decorative brackets
- **Grid of ornamental panels** (cards with embossed borders)
- **Compartmentalized sections** with ruled dividers
- **Symmetrical composition** (classical balance)

## Animations
Restrained, dignified motion:
- Gentle fade-in with upward float (0.6s ease-out)
- Subtle elevation on hover (2px lift)
- Pulsing corner ornaments for pending states
- No bouncing or elastic easing (inappropriate for period)
